Source: PastPages homepage screenshots (pastpages.org, archived on archive.org), read with OCR. 19 publications, US and international: one capture per publication per day, nearest 10am Eastern, May 8 – Nov 7, 2016. "Top stories" is any text in the first 1,200 pixels of the page; "named first" reads down the whole captured page, and when one line names both candidates, whoever comes first in the line wins. Mentions of Melania, Ivanka, Bill or Chelsea, and paid ads, are not counted. The New York Times and Washington Post are not in the archive for this period. NPR was dropped because its 2016 captures rendered without styling; the Denver Post and philly.com have fewer usable days for the same reason, and a capture without enough readable text is left out rather than counted as "no mention".
