Source: GDELT Global Frontpage Graph, which records every link on tens of thousands of news homepages each hour, with its text and its order on the page. One capture per publication per day, at 10am Eastern (or the nearest hour), May 5 – Nov 4, 2024. Only headline links count: menus, topic links, promos and any link text that recurs on five or more days are dropped. "Top stories" is the first 10 headlines on the homepage; "named first" is whichever candidate appears first reading down the headlines, and when one headline names both, whoever comes first in it wins. The opponent is Biden through July 21 (Biden withdrew that afternoon, after the morning capture) and Harris from July 22. Mentions of Trump's family, Hunter or Jill Biden, and other people named Harris are not counted. The Washington Post, Wall Street Journal, USA Today, AP and Reuters are not in the archive; CNN's homepage is missing for part of the period, and a homepage with fewer than 15 headlines is left out rather than counted as "no mention".
